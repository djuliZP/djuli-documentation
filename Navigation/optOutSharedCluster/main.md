# Opt Out of Shared Cluster

Remove your access to a cluster that someone else shared with you.

The cluster, its projects and reports remain in the owner's workspace and continue to exist for everyone else they shared with - opting out only removes your own access. If you change your mind, the owner can re-share the cluster with you.

This button only appears for clusters someone else shared with you. To stop sharing your *own* cluster with someone, use the Share dialog on the cluster owner side.
