# Share Cluster

Grant another Djuli user access to the selected cluster - and, through it, to every project and report inside it.

You choose the recipient by email and the permission level (read, comment, edit). The recipient will see the cluster under the *Shared with me* branch of their navigation tree. Only the cluster owner can change sharing settings.

Sharing a cluster is the fastest way to give a collaborator broad access; share individual reports instead when you only want to expose specific work.
