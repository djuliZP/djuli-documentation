# Alternative Unit

A free-text alternative unit label shown alongside the standard one on plots and analytics — for example, displaying *M* next to *mol/L*, or *ppm* next to *mg/L*.

The conversion between the two is set via [`ConvertionFactor`](../ConvertionFactor) and [`ConvertionOffset`](../ConvertionOffset). Use this when your report has to satisfy two audiences who prefer different conventions (e.g. internal lab vs. external client).
