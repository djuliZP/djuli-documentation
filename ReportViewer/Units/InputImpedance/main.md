# Input Impedance Units

The units Djuli expects the **raw impedance values** to be in, exactly as your instrument exports them (Ω, kΩ, MΩ).

Pick the unit that matches the impedance column in the file. Djuli will then convert internally to whatever you choose under [`OutputImpedance`](../OutputImpedance) for display. See [`Z`](../Z) for the impedance-axis panel as a whole.
