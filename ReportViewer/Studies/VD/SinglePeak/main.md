# Single Peak

When the voltammogram shows more than one peak, restrict the fit to a single one — the one you pick — and ignore the rest.

Use this when only one peak corresponds to your analyte (for example, the redox couple for the target species, while other peaks come from supporting electrolyte, surface oxide, or interferents). The selected peak's position, height or area-under-curve is what feeds the calibration curve, depending on which Peak Fit mode (`PeakPosFit`, `PeakHeightFit`, `PeakAUCFit`) is enabled.
