# Reverse Single Peak

Fit the peak that appears on the **reverse sweep** of a cyclic voltammogram instead of the forward-sweep peak.

In cyclic voltammetry the forward and reverse sweeps each produce a peak (oxidation and reduction respectively). For some analytes the reverse-sweep peak is sharper, more reproducible, or the only one accessible inside the chosen potential window — in that case use Reverse Single to make it the analytical signal feeding the calibration curve.

Pair with [SinglePeak](./SinglePeak) when you need to specify *which* reverse-sweep peak to use.
