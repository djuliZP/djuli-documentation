# Reverse Single

Use the reverse part of a single chrono-amperometric step as the analytical signal — for example, the post-pulse decay current after the applied potential is stepped back.

Useful when the reverse response is more reproducible than the forward one, or when the forward signal is overwhelmed by capacitive (charging) current and the analyte information is cleaner on the reverse step.

If your protocol always uses the forward step, leave this off and Djuli will fit the forward signal as usual.
