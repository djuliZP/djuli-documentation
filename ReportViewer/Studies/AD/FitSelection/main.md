# Fit Selection

Pick the curve model used to fit the amperometric discrete calibration.

Available models include linear, polynomial, sigmoid (4PL / 5PL) and the technique-specific forms Djuli ships. The choice should match what the analyte's response actually looks like across your concentration range — a sigmoid for sensors that saturate, a linear or polynomial fit for the working range of most amperometric/potentiometric assays.

Once selected, the fitted coefficients, R² and LoB/LoD/LoQ are computed against the chosen model and shown in the analytics card.
