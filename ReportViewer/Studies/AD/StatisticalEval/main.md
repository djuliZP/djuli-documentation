# Statistical Evaluation

Enables the analytics card next to the calibration fit. It reports:

- **R²** — goodness of fit, between 0 and 1.
- **Model coefficients** — slope, intercept (and any non-linear parameters) of the fit.
- **SE%** — standard error of each coefficient, expressed as a percentage of the coefficient value.
- **Reciprocal coefficients (C1, C2)** — the inverse form of the model, convenient when you want to predict concentration from a measured current rather than the other way around.
- **LoB / LoD / LoQ** — Limit of Blank, Limit of Detection and Limit of Quantitation, computed from the blank-level noise of the assay.

Required for traceable reports (FDA / CE workflows) and for any external review that needs evidence the calibration is fit-for-purpose.
