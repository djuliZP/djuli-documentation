# Standard Addition

Use the **Standard Addition** method instead of an external calibration curve.

Spike your unknown sample with several known increments of the analyte. Djuli plots the potential response against the *added* concentration, fits a line through it, and extrapolates to the X-intercept — the magnitude of which equals the analyte concentration already present in the unspiked sample.

Especially useful when the sample matrix interferes with the response (e.g. complex food or biological samples) and a clean external calibration curve cannot be trusted.
