# Reverse Single

Use the reverse part of a single chrono-potentiometric step as the analytical signal — for example, the relaxation potential after the applied current is removed.

Useful when the reverse response is more reproducible than the forward one, or when the forward signal includes a transient that the analyte information has not yet emerged from.

If your protocol always uses the forward step, leave this off and Djuli will fit the forward signal as usual.
